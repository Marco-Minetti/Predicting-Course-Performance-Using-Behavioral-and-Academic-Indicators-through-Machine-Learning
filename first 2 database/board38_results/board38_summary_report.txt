Board 38 Placement Research Summary
===================================

Average placed-student salary: 9.21 LPA

1) Placement prediction
Best model: Decision Tree
Accuracy: 0.9998 | MacroF1: 0.9996

2) Salary prediction for placed students
Best model by RMSE: Support Vector Regression
RMSE: 3.4819 | MAE: 3.0099 | R2: -0.0069

3) Job-level prediction
Best model: ECOC
Accuracy: 0.9112 | MacroF1: 0.4138

4) Efficiency-level prediction
Best model: Decision Tree
Accuracy: 0.9998 | MacroF1: 0.6664

5) Efficiency percentage regression
Best model by RMSE: Bagged Trees
RMSE: 4.0681 | MAE: 1.7962 | R2: 0.9584

Main interpretation:
- Placement is highly predictable in this dataset. This may indicate strong signal, synthetic structure, or embedded rules.
- Salary prediction is harder and depends on missing job-market features such as role, company, industry, location, and interview performance.
- Job-level accuracy can be high, but Macro-F1 should be checked because class imbalance can inflate accuracy.
- Feature importance and ablation results should be used to explain why the models work.
- Placement risk scores are useful as a practical output but should not be treated as causal or final decisions.
